The working idea: go-to-market is a system. It has an input (who the product is for, and what event makes them buy) and an output (pipeline a sales team will actually work). Campaigns are experiments inside that system. They are not the system.

When the system is missing, every launch starts from a blank doc. When it exists, a launch is a configuration change:

- Audience, named tightly enough that a rep recognizes the account
- Offer, including what is deliberately out of scope
- Proof, pulled from a source the buyer already trusts
- Handoff, so the next person does not re-interview the lead
