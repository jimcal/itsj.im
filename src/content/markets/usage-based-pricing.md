Usage-based pricing is a clean story: pay for what you consume, expand when value shows up. Procurement still buys seats, because a seat has an owner, a forecast, and a line on a spreadsheet. The companies that announce usage pricing often land on a hybrid — a platform fee, a committed minimum, and a meter nobody reads until renewal.
